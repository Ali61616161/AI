Yola is your AI travel companion. It plans your trip around your real bookings, adapts in the moment and helps you understand and be understood anywhere.

• Upload tickets and hotel bookings — AI reads them and builds your trip
• Day-by-day plans for your budget and travel companions
• "Change the plan": rain, delays or tiredness? Replan the rest of the day in seconds
• Places verified on Google, open-now spots, weather and hotel ideas
• Live two-way conversation translation, text and menu photos
• Works offline: plan, tickets and emergency phrases on your phone
• Expenses and bill splitting, smart packing list, visa info, jet lag plan
• Trip report with ratings and a shareable card
• Optional social mode: meet travelers in public places, safely

Not traveling? Yola works in your own city too:
• My City: plans for tonight and the weekend with real, open places near you
• "Decide for me": can't choose? Get one great pick, new places only if you like
• My lists and City passport: save places, track what you discovered, earn badges
• Getaways: budget-friendly short trips near you, by distance and budget
• Spin the wheel: let luck pick your next country and share the card

Your passport and travel history:
• My passport: see where your passport and extra visas (Schengen, US, UK and more) let you go
• Countries I've visited: your share of the world and each continent, countries, cities and badges
• Schengen 90/180 calculator and passport and visa expiry reminders

Free to start. Yola Pro (monthly or yearly) or a one-time Trip Pass unlocks unlimited planning, replanning and translation. The free version shows ads; Pro is ad-free.
