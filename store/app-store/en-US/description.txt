Yola is your AI travel companion. It plans your trip around your real bookings, adapts in the moment and helps you understand and be understood anywhere.

• Upload tickets and hotel bookings — AI reads them and builds your trip
• Day-by-day plans for your budget and travel companions
• "Change the plan": rain, delays or tiredness? Replan the rest of the day in seconds
• Places verified on Google, open-now spots, weather and hotel ideas
• Live two-way conversation translation, text and menu photos
• Works offline: plan, tickets and emergency phrases on your phone
• Expenses and bill splitting, smart packing list, visa info, jet lag plan
• Trip report with ratings and a shareable card
• Optional social mode: meet travelers in public places, safely

Free to start. Yola Pro (monthly or yearly) or a one-time Trip Pass unlocks unlimited planning, replanning and translation.
