Türkçe

Data controller

Under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"), Dursun Ali Akturan (İstanbul, Türkiye) processes your personal data as data controller as described here. Contact: info@akturangroup.com.

Data we process

• Identity and contact: email address, display name, birth year.

• Travel profile: citizenship, types of extra visas and residence permits, home city (city level).

• Customer transactions: trips, booking documents and the details read from them, plans, expenses, saved and visited places, subscription status.

• Location: only while you use features that need it.

• Images and audio: document images, photos and speech transcripts you choose to translate.

• Security: session and error logs.

• Marketing: email preferences, only with your consent.

Purposes and legal bases

Providing the app and performing our contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c), legal obligations (Art. 5/2-ç), legitimate interests such as security and improving the service (Art. 5/2-f) and your explicit consent for location-based features, social mode and marketing emails (Art. 5/1), which you can withdraw at any time.

Transfers

Data is shared with the service providers listed in the Privacy Policy and with authorities when required by law. Some providers are abroad (for example in the United States); transfers abroad are made with appropriate safeguards under KVKK Art. 9 (standard contracts notified to the Authority).

Collection method

Electronically through the mobile app, from the information you enter, the documents you upload and the device features you allow.

Your rights and applications

You have the rights listed in KVKK Art. 11, including to learn whether your data is processed, request information, correction, deletion and notification of third parties, object to results of solely automated analysis and claim compensation. Write to info@akturangroup.com from the email address registered in the app; requests are answered free of charge within 30 days.
