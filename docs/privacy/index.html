Privacy Policy

Türkçe

Who we are

Yola is provided by Dursun Ali Akturan (Akturan Group markası), İstanbul, Türkiye ("we"). We are the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: info@akturangroup.com. Effective date: 2026-10-12.

What we collect

• Account: your email address and, if you choose, a display name, birth year and a short bio (needed only for social mode).

• Travel profile: your citizenship(s), the visas or residence permits you choose to add and your home city (stored only at city level, about 1 km). We use them to show where you can travel, to filter the destination wheel and for My City suggestions.

• Places: places you save to your lists and places you mark as visited (your city passport).

• Trips: destinations, dates, travel preferences, plans, expenses, packing lists, ratings and notes you create.

• Documents you upload: tickets and booking confirmations (images or PDFs) and the details read from them.

• Location: only while you use features that need it (places open near you, changing your plan, emergency sharing, social mode). In social mode only an approximate location (about 1 km) is stored.

• Translation: the text, the speech transcript and the photos you choose to translate. Speech is turned into text by your phone’s speech recognition service.

• Purchases: your subscription status and store transaction identifiers (we never see your card details).

• Social mode: invites, messages, blocks and reports.

• Technical data needed to run the service, such as error logs. On the free plan, Google AdMob may process advertising data as described under Advertising.

How we use it

To provide the app: reading your bookings, planning and replanning your trips, suggesting evening plans and getaways near your city, estimating trip costs, showing visa requirements for your passport, verifying places, showing weather, translating, keeping your trips available offline, processing subscriptions, keeping social mode safe and answering your requests. We use your ratings to tailor future plans. We do not sell your personal data. With your separate, optional consent we may email you news and offers; you can withdraw it at any time in the app or with the link in every email.

Legal bases

Performance of our contract with you (providing the app), your consent (location, social mode — you can withdraw it at any time in the app), our legitimate interests (security, preventing abuse, improving the service) and legal obligations (for example tax records for purchases).

Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us run Yola, under contracts that require them to protect it:

• Supabase (database, authentication, file storage, server functions)

• Google (Gemini AI for reading documents, planning and photo translation; Maps Platform for places and weather; Cloud Translation)

• RevenueCat, Apple App Store and Google Play (purchases and subscriptions)

• Your phone’s speech recognition provider (Apple or Google) for conversation translation

• Google AdMob (ads, free plan only)

• Booking and ticket partners (such as Booking.com, GetYourGuide, Tiqets and flight or bus ticket sites) only when you tap their link: we pass the destination and dates in the link, never your account details.

Visa and passport information comes from public sources (Passport Index Data, passportindex.org, and official government pages); no personal data is sent to them.

Some providers process data outside your country, including in the United States, with appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Advertising

The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob; paid plans have no ads. AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier, approximate location (from your IP address) and ad interactions to show and measure ads. On iOS we only use the advertising identifier if you allow tracking; in the EU, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first and you can change it in the app. Optional rewarded ads give you an extra daily use; for that, Google sends us a signed confirmation with your account ID. We never share your trips, documents or messages with advertisers.

What other travelers see

Only if you turn on social mode: your display name, short bio, interests, what you are up for, verification status and an approximate distance. Your exact location, email and birth year are never shown. You can hide yourself, block or report anyone at any time.

How long we keep data

As long as your account exists. Offline copies on your phone are removed when you sign out. When you delete your account in Profile → Delete account, your account, trips, documents, messages and other personal data are deleted immediately, except records we must keep by law (such as purchase records kept by the app stores).

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, withdraw consent and object to processing. Most of this can be done in the app; for anything else write to info@akturangroup.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye: KVKK; in the EU: your local supervisory authority).

Children

Yola is not directed to children under 13. Social mode is only for people aged 18 and over.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to every record is restricted to its owner by database rules, and uploaded documents are stored in private folders.

Changes

We will tell you in the app before material changes take effect.
